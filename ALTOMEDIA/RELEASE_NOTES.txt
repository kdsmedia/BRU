BERUANG v1.2.0 — Perbaikan & Penyempurnaan Iklan (AdMob)

Pembaruan penting yang memastikan iklan Google AdMob tampil dan
berfungsi dengan sempurna pada perangkat Android.

Perbaikan di v1.2.0:
- Iklan banner kini benar-benar muncul di bagian bawah layar.
  Sebelumnya banner sering tidak tampil karena status pemuatan
  tidak dipantau; kini sistem mendeteksi keberhasilan/kegagalan
  muat iklan dan mencoba ulang otomatis bila terjadi gangguan
  jaringan atau "no fill" sementara.
- Iklan interstisial (layar penuh) kini lebih andal: iklan
  dimuat lebih awal dan dimuat ulang otomatis setelah ditutup,
  sehingga siap ditampilkan pada perpindahan layar (jarak
  minimum 15 menit antar tayangan tetap berlaku).
- Iklan reward (rewarded video) kini stabil: perbaikan manajemen
  listener agar pemberian kuota bonus (+1 posting/komentar)
  selalu terdeteksi dan tidak menggangu iklan interstisial
  berikutnya.
- Inisialisasi SDK AdMob kini idempoten dan aman dari panggilan
  bersamaan, mencegah kondisi balapan (race condition) saat aplikasi
  dibuka.
- Mode uji iklan: developer dapat memverifikasi tampilan iklan
  dengan menambahkan ?adtest=1 (atau localStorage.adtest='1') untuk
  memakai unit iklan contoh resmi Google—tidak menimbulkan
  impresi/pendapatan iklan nyata.
- Catatan status AdMob kini tampil pada panel debug (?debug=1)
  untuk memudahkan pengecekan.

Catatan teknis:
- Android minSdk 23 (Android 6.0+) hingga targetSdk 37.
- Plugin @capacitor-community/admob v6.

BERUANG v1.2.0 — AdMob reliability & polish

Important update ensuring Google AdMob ads display and function
correctly on Android devices.

Fixes in v1.2.0:
- Banner ads now actually appear at the bottom of the screen.
  Previously the banner often failed to show because load status
  was not monitored; the app now detects ad load success/failure
  and automatically retries on transient network or "no fill"
  errors so the banner self-heals.
- Interstitial (full-screen) ads are more reliable: they are
  preloaded earlier and automatically reloaded after dismissal,
  ready to show on screen changes (the 15-minute minimum gap
  between impressions still applies).
- Rewarded video ads are stable: listener management was fixed so
  the +1 post/comment quota grant is always detected and no longer
  breaks subsequent interstitial reloads.
- AdMob SDK initialization is now idempotent and re-entrant safe,
  preventing race conditions on app launch.
- Ad test mode: developers can verify ad rendering with ?adtest=1
  (or localStorage.adtest='1'), which uses Google's official sample
  ad-unit IDs and never produces real impressions/revenue.
- AdMob status is now surfaced in the debug panel (?debug=1) for
  easier troubleshooting.

Technical notes:
- Android minSdk 23 (Android 6.0+) up to targetSdk 37.
- Plugin @capacitor-community/admob v6.

BERUANG v1.1.0 — Feature update & full localization

Major update with new features and full Indonesian localization
of the entire user interface.

New in v1.1.0:
- Clickable links in posts, comments, and chat messages. URLs
  (http/https) written in captions, comments, or messages are
  automatically turned into clickable links that open the
  device's system browser.
- Admin can pin posts to the top of the feed, with a "Pinned"
  badge and pin/unpin controls.
- Chat button on profile visits to start a private conversation
  directly from another user's profile.
- Delete post (owner or admin) with a delete control on each
  post.
- Delete individual chat messages and clear an entire
  conversation (trash icon in chat header).

Other changes:
- The entire user interface is now in Bahasa Indonesia (auth
  screen, navigation, feed, comments, chat, points wallet,
  transfer, notifications, settings, info modals, and toasts).
- Minor fixes and stability improvements.

Welcome to BERUANG, a lightweight and entertaining social app

BAHASA INDONESIA
------------------------------------------------------------
BERUANG v2.4.4 — Story 24 Jam Otomatis & Preview Popup

Pembaruan v2.4.4 memperbaiki fitur Story sesuai keluhan pengguna.

Yang diperbaiki di v2.4.4:
- STORY OTOMATIS TERHAPUS SETELAH 24 JAM: story kini dibatasi usia 24
  jam. Pembersihan berjalan otomatis di latar (saat feed dimuat) sehingga
  story kedaluwarsa dihapus untuk semua pengguna, dan daftar story hanya
  menampilkan story yang masih berlaku (< 24 jam).
- PREVIEW STORY DENGAN POPUP: sebelumnya mengetuk story tidak melakukan
  apa-apa ("view story" belum diimplementasikan). Kini mengetuk story
  membukanya dalam popup layar penuh yang sama seperti preview gambar
  postingan — dengan pinch-to-zoom, double-tap zoom, dan tombol tutup.

Catatan teknis:
- Android minSdk 23 (Android 6.0+) sampai targetSdk/compileSdk 37.
- Dibangun dengan Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, Supabase Kotlin 3.0.0,
  Coil (gambar), AdMob. Backend AI: Supabase Edge Function (Groq).
- Backend: Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime).
- Build dirilis dengan keystore ALTOMEDIA.jks (alias kdsmedia).

Catatan:
- Aplikasi gratis dengan iklan.
- Dukungan: altomediaindonesia@gmail.com.

ENGLISH — v2.4.4
------------------------------------------------------------
BERUANG v2.4.4 — 24-Hour Stories & Popup Preview

The v2.4.4 update fixes Story behavior based on user reports.

Fixed in v2.4.4:
- STORIES AUTO-DELETED AFTER 24 HOURS: stories are now limited to 24
  hours. Cleanup runs automatically in the background (when the feed
  loads), so expired stories are removed for everyone, and the stories
  list only shows still-valid stories (< 24 hours).
- STORY PREVIEW WITH A POPUP: previously tapping a story did nothing
  (the "view story" action was not implemented). Tapping a story now
  opens it in a full-screen popup identical to the post-image preview —
  with pinch-to-zoom, double-tap zoom, and a close button.

Technical notes:
- Android minSdk 23 (Android 6.0+) up to targetSdk/compileSdk 37.
- Built with Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, Supabase Kotlin 3.0.0,
  Coil (images), AdMob. AI backend: Supabase Edge Function (Groq).
- Backend: Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime).
- Release signed with ALTOMEDIA.jks (alias kdsmedia).

Notes:
- Free app with ads.
- Support: altomediaindonesia@gmail.com.
